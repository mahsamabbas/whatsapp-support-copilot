# Acme Shop Customer Support Policy

Thank you for shopping with Acme Shop. This policy explains how we handle common support requests for online orders.

## Returns

You may return most unused items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in original packaging with tags attached. Opened hygiene or personalized products are not eligible unless defective. To start a return, contact support with your order number; we will email a prepaid return label when applicable.

## Shipping

Standard shipping typically arrives in **3–5 business days** after the order leaves our warehouse. Express options may be available at checkout. You will receive tracking information by email once the carrier scans your package. Delays caused by weather or carrier issues are outside our control, but we will help you follow up with the carrier.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after we receive and inspect returned goods. Processing usually takes **5–10 business days** after approval, depending on your bank or card issuer. Partial refunds may apply if items are damaged or missing components.

## Order Tracking

Use the tracking link in your shipment email or sign in to your account to view status. If tracking has not updated for 48 hours, contact us with your order number so we can investigate with the carrier.

## Support Hours

Live chat and email support are available **Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM** (local time, excluding public holidays). Messages received outside these hours are answered on the next business day.
